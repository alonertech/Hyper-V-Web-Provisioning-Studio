# Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally directional. Community feedback may change priorities.

## V3.1 — Current

- VM modification
- Virtual Switch management tab
- Readable job/audit presentation
- Professional embedded management store
- Templates and policies
- RBAC
- Tamper-evident audit chain

## Near term

- More detailed VM hardware modification
- Better host health and monitoring views
- Expanded remote-host diagnostics
- Safer/clearer change previews for networking operations
- Improved installer and upgrade experience

## Enterprise direction

- Centralized multi-host management
- Enterprise authentication/SSO
- Stronger approval workflows
- SQL Server-backed management store
- Centralized audit/SIEM integration
- Cluster-aware workflows
- Signed MSI and signed PowerShell distribution
- Formal enterprise security and compliance documentation

Roadmap items are not guarantees of schedule or final implementation.
